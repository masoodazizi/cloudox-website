Understanding a new cloud environment shouldn't take days.

That's the simple bet behind CloudoX: that consultants and cloud engineers walking into unfamiliar AWS environments deserve better than a console crawl, a stack of screenshots, and a hand-rolled diagram nobody trusts.

## Why now

Cloud teams keep facing the same problem from different angles:

- Consultants land on a new engagement and lose the first week to discovery.
- Engineers inherit an environment without diagrams, runbooks, or context.
- Pre-sales teams need to qualify opportunities without the time to actually understand them.
- Platform teams audit their own AWS Organization and find tribal knowledge instead of documentation.

Generic AI tools haven't solved this. They invent architecture, fabricate ownership, and produce documents that nobody is willing to sign.

## What CloudoX is

CloudoX is a structured cloud discovery and documentation platform. The MVP focuses on AWS:

- Multi-account AWS Organizations
- Multi-region discovery
- A typed knowledge graph with explicit relationships
- Deterministic interpretation with confidence and evidence
- Stakeholder-ready Markdown discovery reports

The architecture is intentionally cloud-shaped, not AWS-shaped — Azure and GCP support are next on the roadmap.

## What CloudoX is *not*

We're being deliberate about the line:

- Not a CMDB.
- Not a security scanner.
- Not a diagram-only tool.

CloudoX does one thing: it helps you understand a cloud environment quickly, with output you can defend.
