Facts on their own don't tell you what to do. CloudoX turns interpreted knowledge into **Knowledge Intelligence** — a single, ranked set of evidence-grounded items generated once per environment, then consumed by every view.

## What intelligence looks like

Intelligence items are normalized into one shape and cover several kinds:

- Findings and risks
- Recommendations and modernization opportunities
- Cost opportunities
- Operational concerns
- Security exposures and dependency concerns
- Validation questions and evidence gaps

Each item is **evidence-grounded** (or is itself an explicit evidence gap), carries a confidence and a severity that are reused from the knowledge model rather than invented, and is ranked by significance. Leadership escalation is a cross-cutting flag, so the items that need a decision rise to the top of the Executive view.

Because intelligence is generated **once** and shared, every Knowledge View selects and prioritizes from the same set — the Security view surfaces exposures, the FinOps view surfaces cost opportunities — without re-deriving "what matters" per audience.

## Knowledge Quality

A report can be perfectly grounded and still be useless to a CTO — too much inventory, no prioritization, no decisions. CloudoX measures **usefulness**, not only correctness.

> Correctness asks "is it true?" Knowledge Quality also asks "is it useful to the audience that will read it?"

Every Knowledge View is scored deterministically across dimensions such as:

- **Persona coverage** — does the view answer its audience's questions?
- **Signal-to-noise** — is the important content buried under inventory?
- **Actionability** — does it tell the reader what to do?
- **Prioritization** — is the most significant content surfaced first?

The deterministic score is the authoritative one. An **optional AI quality review** can critique a view on top of that — but it never changes the facts, never invents intelligence, and never overrides the score. It simply tells you where a view is thin, noisy, or missing something its audience needs.

## Why it's separate from the report

Intelligence and quality live in the knowledge foundation, not inside any single report. That means the same ranked intelligence powers reports, the dashboard, and future channels — and the quality signal travels with the knowledge, not with one document.
